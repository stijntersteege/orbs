# Code of Conduct

Orbs follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want Orbs to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

**Do**

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Credit others for their ideas and contributions.

**Do not**

- Harass, insult or demean anyone, in public or in private.
- Use sexualized language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances.
- Publish anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Keep going after someone has asked you to stop.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers at **[conduct email]**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers who are involved in a report step aside from handling it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they find appropriate, from a private warning to a permanent ban, following the [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Contributor Covenant.
